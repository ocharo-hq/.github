# Ocharo

*Last updated: 2026-09-29*

Clothing, textiles and stories from Uzbekistan.

Ocharo is Maftuna Tog'ayeva's clothing brand, based in Tashkent.

Our planned digital portfolio brings together four experiences:

- Uzbekistan's textile culture, visual collections and stories.
- Practical guides to what to wear, where and when.
- Ocharo collection news and work from the atelier.
- A catalogue of Ocharo garments and materials.

This GitHub organization is the home being established for that work.
